1. Quantitative Research & Backtesting Architecture

Reliable quantitative alpha generation requires separating signal from noise while strictly preventing common backtest fallacies (overfitting, survivorship bias, and lookahead leakage). Ankit's research framework applies statistical rigor to historical tick and OHLCV datasets.

  • Stationarity & Volatility: Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) testing, cointegration modeling, and volatility clustering analysis using GARCH models.
  • Execution Friction Simulator: Models bid-ask spread consumption, exchange tier fees, and linear market impact penalties to ensure strategy viability under real liquidity conditions.
  • Risk Budgeting: Enforces strict Maximum Drawdown (MDD) caps, Value-at-Risk (VaR) monitoring, and volatility-scaled position sizing.
